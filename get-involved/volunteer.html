Become a Buddy

Come play. Be a buddy.

Every other Friday, we host badminton nights where neurodivergent (ND) kids come to play. They need buddies — kids and teens who'll rally, laugh, and be a friend for the evening.

No experience needed. If you can hold a racket and be patient, supportive and stay engaged, you qualify. 2 volunteer hours per Friday, tracked.

Upcoming Fridays: Oct 9 • Oct 23 • Nov 6 • Nov 20 • Dec 4 • Dec 18

Volunteers under 18 sign up with a parent/guardian's contact.
