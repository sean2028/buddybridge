The BuddyBridge Video Series

Kindness is easier to practice when we have words for it. These eight short captioned videos give students concrete phrases for real situations — at school, in clubs, and online.

1

Understanding Neurodivergence (3:44)

Assumptions → understanding. "Different brain, not a broken brain."

2

Big Feelings, Different Expressions (2:39)

Misbehavior → overwhelm. Give space; fewer words; help after the storm.

3

Respectful Communication (2:29)

One "right" way → flexible communication. Wait; ask before helping; offer choices.

4

Kindness Counts Online Too (2:58)

Read twice, send once; screenshots last forever; no one gets skipped.

5

Inclusion in Practice (3:02)

One-time invite → genuine connection. Invite like we mean it; change the game, not the kid.

6

Be the Person Who Helps (2:28)

Notice, interrupt, include, report. Don't be the audience.

7

How to Make It Right (2:22)

Say what you did; don't rush forgiveness; show change.

8

From Access to Belonging (2:38)

Access → belonging. Show up again; remember their name; keep the door open.
