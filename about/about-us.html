About Us

BuddyBridge was started in 2021 by two brothers, Daniel and Sean. It began with a group of neurodivergent (ND) kids from our local church community — every other Friday, we host badminton nights at the church.

We've grown up alongside our ND friends: getting to know them and their families, seeing the world from their perspective. They've helped us become better, more mindful people.

Watching them grow is what keeps us going — and what drives us to keep making an impact on our community. We want to help more people understand neurodivergent kids, and help our community become a better, warmer place.

We welcome more friends to join BuddyBridge and grow with us.

The Power of Kindness — Summer 2017: We once trained with a world badminton champion. Despite all she had achieved, she treated us like younger brothers — playing with us, encouraging us, always smiling. Her kindness lit a candle in us. Now we hope to pass that light forward.
