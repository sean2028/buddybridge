Advocacy

Bring belonging to your school.

BuddyBridge's 8-video series is a ready-made advocacy toolkit for schools, clubs, and communities. Each short video (2–4 minutes) teaches one idea about understanding, including, and standing up for neurodivergent (ND) peers — built around concrete phrases kids can actually use.

How to use it: play a video at a club meeting or in class, talk about the phrases together, and try them out in real life.

Watch the Video Series

Start here

1. Pick a video

Choose the unit that fits your group — start with Understanding Neurodivergence.

2. Watch together

Play it at a club meeting or in class. Two to four minutes is all it takes.

3. Try the phrases

Talk about the five phrases together, then try them out in real life that week.
